From your first prompt to your first creation

  1. Copy your prompt. Open the Gemini Prompt Lab from your lesson dashboard and copy the prompt you want to try.
  2. Open Gemini and create. Click Open Gemini, paste your copied prompt into Gemini, and generate your image.
  3. Return and complete your lesson. Save your prompt in meuseAI to earn 250 lesson XP.
  4. Post your image and submit the link. Share your creation publicly with #meuseAI, then paste the public post link in your lesson’s share form. Earn another 250 XP after admin approval, once per lesson.

THE 4-DAY JOURNEY

Build one image-making skill each day

Day 1 starts after email verification. Each next day opens at least 24 hours later, after you finish the previous lesson.

DAY 01

Describe your scene

+250 XP

Learn to describe a subject, its surroundings and the camera view. Choose a theme or adapt a prompt to your own idea.

Exercise: Create a scene, then change one detail and compare the result.
DAY 02

Shape light and mood

+250 XP

See how lighting, shadows and materials change an image. Practise giving clear instructions for the look you want.

Exercise: Try the same subject with different lighting and textures.
DAY 03

Keep a consistent character

+250 XP

Practise repeating the details that identify your character across scenes. Compare results and refine your instructions when details change.

Exercise: Generate your signature character in two distinct camera angles and actions.
DAY 04

Refine your final image

+250 XP

Bring the skills together and improve your image with follow-up instructions. Complete all four lessons to earn 1,000 lesson XP, then explore what to learn next.

Exercise: Make a sequence of edits in Gemini, then save the prompt you used to complete the lesson.

Yes, 100% free with no credit card required. You get all 4 lessons, exercises, XP tracking, and learning hub access with just your name and email.

Each lesson is designed for busy schedules: roughly 5 minutes of focused reading and 10 minutes of hands-on prompt creation in Gemini.
