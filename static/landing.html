THE 4-DAY JOURNEY

Build one image-making skill each day

Day 1 starts after email verification. Finish a lesson and the next one opens immediately, so you can learn at your own pace.

DAY 01

Describe your scene

+250 XP

Learn to describe a subject, its surroundings and the camera view. Choose a theme or adapt a prompt to your own idea.

Exercise: Create a scene, then change one detail and compare the result.
DAY 02

Shape light and mood

+250 XP

See how lighting, shadows and materials change an image. Practise giving clear instructions for the look you want.

Exercise: Try the same subject with different lighting and textures.
DAY 03

Keep a consistent character

+250 XP

Practise repeating the details that identify your character across scenes. Compare results and refine your instructions when details change.

Exercise: Generate your signature character in two distinct camera angles and actions.
DAY 04

Refine your final image

+250 XP

Bring the skills together and improve your image with follow-up instructions. Complete all four lessons to earn 1,000 lesson XP, with +50 momentum XP when you keep going on the same day.

Exercise: Make a sequence of edits in Gemini, then save the prompt you used to complete the lesson.
