1. Silent Time-Based Drip Failure (Fixed in v2)

Production Alignment

The Fix: Standardized on Paystack (₦5,000 Tier 1 / ₦25,000 Tier 2). Aligns with your existing production stack on CRDLedger and ensures direct local bank settlement without cross-border FX drag.
